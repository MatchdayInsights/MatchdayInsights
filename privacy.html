MATCHDAY INSIGHTS

Privacy

For the website matchdayinsights.com and the Matchday Insights app. Last updated November 2026.

In short: there are no accounts and we never ask for your name or email. The clubs you follow stay on your phone. If you turn on alerts, we keep only what's needed to send them. We don't sell anything about you, and there are no ads.

What stays on your device

  • The clubs and national teams you follow.
  • Small settings, like light or dark mode, how a list is sorted, and whether you closed a tip.

These are saved in your browser or the app on your phone, not by us. Clearing the app's or the site's data removes them.

Alerts

If you turn on alerts (goals, full time, kick-off, ranking moves), we store your phone's push address (a code your phone's browser or system gives us for sending notifications) and the list of teams you follow. They're kept on our alerts service, which runs on Cloudflare, and are used only to send you those alerts. Turn alerts off and both are deleted. If your phone tells us the push address no longer works, it's deleted too.

Visitor numbers

We use Google Analytics to count visits: which pages are read, roughly where from (country or city), and the kind of device and browser. It uses cookies and Google's own rules apply: Google's privacy policy. We use it to see what people read, nothing else.

Pictures and live scores from other services

To show them, your device loads some things straight from other companies, which then see your device's internet address (IP address), like any website you visit:

  • Player photos: API-Sports (the football data we use). Club crests are on our own site.
  • Flags: jsDelivr. Fonts: Google Fonts.
  • Live scores: our own live-scores service on Cloudflare. It doesn't keep who asked.
  • The site itself is hosted by GitHub Pages.

Children

Matchday Insights is a football statistics site for a general audience. It isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.

Odds

We show our own chances for each match and, next to them, bookmakers' average odds for comparison. There's no betting in the app or on the site, and no links to bookmakers.

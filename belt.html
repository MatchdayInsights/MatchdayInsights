The world title belt

Boxing has a lineal champion: to be the man, you beat the man. Club football never had one - so we made it. The belt starts with the world's top-rated club at the start of the 2021-22 European season (1 Jul 2021). Whoever beats the holder takes it - in any league or cup, anywhere in the world. A draw keeps it, and so does a penalty shoot-out.

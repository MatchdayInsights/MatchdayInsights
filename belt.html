The world title belt

Boxing has a lineal champion: to be the man, you beat the man. Club football never had one - so we made it. The belt started with the world's best club when our record begins, on 25 Jan 2020. Whoever beats the holder takes it - in any league or cup, anywhere in the world. A draw keeps it, and so does a penalty shoot-out.

Reporting something wrong

The data covers thousands of clubs across every tracked division, and errors do get through. These are the most useful things to flag:

  • A club in the wrong country or league. Clubs that only ever appear in continental competition are the usual culprits — there is no domestic league result to place them from.
  • A club that should have dropped out, or should be included. Relegations out of the tracked pyramid are the hardest case to catch automatically.
  • A missing competition or season.
  • A result that never registered. Occasionally a match sits in the feed marked as still in progress long after full time, and never gets counted.

Including the club name and the competition makes it much quicker to track down.
